# Shipping and Delivery

This document describes delivery options for Larkspur Home Appliances (a fictional company used for demonstration).

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery takes 5 business days and costs 15 USD per order. Orders above 500 USD qualify for free standard delivery. Deliveries are made on weekdays between 8 and 18 local time.

## Express delivery

Express delivery arrives within 2 business days and costs 45 USD per order. Express orders must be placed before 14 local time to be dispatched the same day. Express delivery is not available for items above 80 kg.

## Installation service

Professional installation is available for washing machines, dishwashers and ovens at a fee of 60 USD per appliance. Installation is scheduled within 3 business days after delivery. The installer removes the old appliance free of charge when the customer requests it at booking time.

## Delivery tracking

A tracking link is sent by email within 24 hours after the order is dispatched. The link shows the carrier, the current location and a delivery window of 4 hours. Customers can reschedule a delivery up to 12 hours before the delivery window starts.

## Missed delivery

If nobody is available to receive the order, the carrier makes a second attempt on the next business day. After 2 failed attempts the order is returned to the warehouse and a redelivery fee of 20 USD applies. Orders that are not rescheduled within 10 days are cancelled and refunded minus the delivery charge.
