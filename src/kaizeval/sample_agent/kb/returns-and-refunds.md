# Returns and Refunds

This document describes how Larkspur Home Appliances (a fictional company used for demonstration) handles returns and refunds.

## Refund window

Customers may return an unused appliance within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. Opened but undamaged items can be returned within 14 days and are subject to a 10% restocking fee. Items returned after these periods are not eligible for a refund. The delivery date is the date shown on the signed delivery note.

## Refund processing time

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item passes inspection. Bank transfers can take up to 3 additional business days to appear on a statement. Customers receive an email confirmation as soon as the refund is issued.

## Return shipping costs

Return shipping is free when the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was delivered. In all other cases the customer pays a flat return fee of 25 USD per appliance. Oversized items above 80 kg require a scheduled pickup, which is arranged by the support team.

## Non-returnable items

Water filters, light bulbs and other consumable parts cannot be returned once the sealed packaging is opened. Gift cards and installation services are also non-returnable. Clearance items marked as final sale are excluded from the return policy.

## Damaged on arrival

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with at least 2 photographs of the item and its packaging. A replacement is shipped within 5 business days once the report is approved. If no replacement is in stock, the customer receives a full refund including the original shipping charge.
