# Warranty and Repairs

This document describes warranty coverage for Larkspur Home Appliances (a fictional company used for demonstration).

## Warranty period

Every appliance is covered by a manufacturer warranty of 24 months from the delivery date. Compressors in refrigerators and freezers are covered for 60 months. The warranty applies only to household use and is void for commercial use.

## Warranty exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage caused by incorrect installation, power surges or unauthorised repairs. Cosmetic damage such as scratches and dents is excluded unless it is reported within 48 hours of delivery. Consumable parts such as filters and seals are covered for 6 months only.

## Repair process

A repair request is answered within 2 business days and a technician visit is scheduled within 7 business days. If the appliance cannot be repaired within 21 days, the customer may choose a replacement or a refund of the purchase price. Repairs under warranty are free of charge, including parts and labour.

## Extended warranty

An extended warranty adds 36 months of coverage and must be purchased within 30 days of delivery. The price is 12% of the appliance purchase price. The extended warranty can be transferred once to a new owner for a fee of 20 USD.

## Out-of-warranty repairs

Repairs outside the warranty period are charged at 75 USD per technician visit plus the cost of parts. A written quote is provided before any work begins and is valid for 14 days. Repaired parts carry a separate guarantee of 90 days.
